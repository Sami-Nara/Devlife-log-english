1. Introduction

Our names are Sarly Samira Naranjo Tello and Emmanuel Paz Cabezas, and we are presenting this DevLife Log as a team. We have been studying Systems Engineering at Universidad del Pacífico in Buenaventura, and we have been learning about programming, software development, and technology.

We are using BeautyTurns, a mobile application for small beauty businesses, as our project example. The Present Perfect Progressive (have/has + been + verb-ing) is important for our professional training because it helps us explain the activities and projects we have been working on over time. We have been improving our technical and English skills, and we want to continue learning and developing as Systems Engineers.

2. Justification

Pedagogical

We have been learning English while we have been studying Systems Engineering. The Present Perfect Progressive has been helping us describe activities that started in the past and have continued until now. We have been using this grammar to talk about our learning process and our technology projects.

Professional

We have been developing our technical skills through different projects. In BeautyTurns, we have been working on a mobile application for small beauty businesses. Using English has also been helping us prepare for technical documentation and communication in our future careers.

Local

We have been studying in Buenaventura, where access to technology can sometimes be difficult. We have been learning through university projects and independent practice. These experiences have been helping us understand how technology can be useful for people and small businesses in our community.

3. Objectives

General objective

To demonstrate our English communication skills by creating a digital DevLife Log about our learning process in Systems Engineering and our work on the BeautyTurns project, using the Present Perfect Progressive.

Specific objectives

  1. To use the Present Perfect Progressive to describe activities and projects that we have been working on during our studies.
  2. To learn and use technical vocabulary related to programming, software development, and databases.
  3. To describe our experience and progress as Systems Engineering students through English sentences and technical examples.
  4. To improve our ability to communicate our technical experience in English through the BeautyTurns project.
